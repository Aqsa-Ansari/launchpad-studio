LAUNCHPAD STUDIO - HOUSE BRAND & CLAIMS GUIDELINES

TONE
- Confident, warm, concrete. Prefer verbs and numbers over adjectives.
- Write for one person, not "users". Short sentences. No jargon without a reason.
- Never shout: no ALL CAPS words, at most one exclamation mark per piece.

POSITIONING
- Every launch needs one sentence in the form: "For <audience> who <need>, <product> is the <category> that <key benefit>, unlike <alternative>."
- Lead with the customer problem, not the feature list.

CLAIMS POLICY (compliance)
- Never promise outcomes: avoid "guaranteed", "100% safe", "risk-free", "cures", "miracle", "clinically proven" unless a cited study is attached.
- Comparative claims ("better than X", "#1", "best in the world") need a named, dated source.
- Health, safety, environmental ("eco-friendly", "carbon neutral") and performance claims must be specific and verifiable.
- Never include personal data (names, emails, phone numbers) of individual customers or reviewers.

CHANNELS
- Prefer 3-5 channels with one measurable KPI each.
- Always include one owned channel (email, site), one earned channel (PR, community) and one paid test.

CALL TO ACTION
- One clear action, one deadline or reason to act now, written as an imperative.
